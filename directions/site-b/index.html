Questions

What is Halves?

A dating app for New Yorkers aged 21–39. We introduce you only when the fit works both ways, and each introduction comes with a reason. We’re new, and we’re building it with our first members.

What does “both ways” mean?

Most apps ask whether someone matches what you want. We also ask whether you match what they want. If it only fits one side, we don’t introduce you.

Why is it called Halves? Do I need someone to complete me?

No. You’re whole. A match has two halves: your side and theirs. The name is about the fit, not about you.

When does Halves open?

We’re starting with a small founding pool in New York and opening in groups. We’ll email you when there’s room for you. We don’t have a date yet, and we won’t make one up.

What does it cost?

Joining the waitlist is free. We’ll tell you the price before you pay anything, and nobody will be charged without clear notice of the amount, the renewal date, and how to cancel.

What do you do with my information?

We ask for the minimum: your email, first name, age and ZIP, so we can build balanced groups. No ads. We never sell your information. See the privacy notice.

How do you keep people safe?

We’re building verification and reporting before we open, and we’ll publish exactly what we check and what we don’t. Verification confirms identity, not intentions. Until it’s live, there’s nothing to join but a waitlist, and no one is introduced to anyone.

Will you really only introduce a few people?

Yes. A few at a time, each with a reason, instead of an endless feed. We’ll tell you the pace when we open.

Why New York?

We’re starting with one group of New Yorkers, kept in balance, so introductions can work for both sides. We’ll tell you if that changes.
