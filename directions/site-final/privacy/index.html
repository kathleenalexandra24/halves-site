Privacy

Last updated October 24, 2026

What happens to the information you give us when you join the Halves waitlist.

What we collect

  • Your email address, first and last name, age and ZIP code.
  • How you found us, if your link included a tag, and when you joined and agreed to this notice.

How we use it

To tell you when Halves opens, to invite early users, and to understand who is joining.

What we do not do

We do not sell your information or share your sign-up details. Only the Halves team can see the waitlist.

How to be removed

Email hello@meethalves.com from the address you signed up with. We will delete your sign-up and reply to confirm. Every launch email will also tell you how to unsubscribe.

When the Halves app opens

The app will ask for more, such as your survey answers. It will show you its own privacy notice and ask for your consent first. Joining the waitlist does not create an account.

Age

Halves is being built for adults ages 18+.

Questions

Email hello@meethalves.com.
