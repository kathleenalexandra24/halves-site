type (free / open, OFL)

Bricolage Grotesque

Display + wordmark. Bold 700, tight. Headlines ≤ 12 words.

Instrument Sans for UI and body.

Body 400/500. Mono: JetBrains Mono for “reason” labels.

motion principle: “fit”

  • Everything arrives in two parts and meets in the middle (cards, buttons, transitions).
  • Ease-in-out, 320 to 600 ms. No bounce, no confetti.
  • The mark animates once: halves slide in, seam holds a beat.
  • Reduced motion: cross-fade only.

photography and illustration

photography
  • Real New Yorkers, 21-39, unposed, two people never staring at camera.
  • Natural light, street scale, one subject per frame, bone/ink color grade.
  • Pairs: diptychs, two frames, one story. Seam in the middle.
illustration
  • Flat D-shapes only: semicircles, offset, 2 colors max per piece.
  • Used for empty states, reports, stickers. No faces, no hearts.

voice, tone, tagline

Plain. Warm. Dry. Specific. Honest about being new.

Fixing “incomplete”: never use other half, better half, complete you. Say once: You’re whole. A match has two halves.

Tone shifts: confident in ads, quiet in safety, funny in empty states.

tagline options

  • Fit goes both ways. (recommended, matches existing)
  • Two yeses.
  • Matched both ways.
  • Each half counts.
  • Introduced for a reason.
  • Whole already. Better together is not a claim.
