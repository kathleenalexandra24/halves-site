01 / NAME

Why rename: a half is not a whole.

"Half/Halves" is a crowded namespace and can read as incompleteness, a thing the copy has to defend against (the platform doc bans 'other half' for this reason). We looked for a name that carries reciprocal fit with a stated reason on its own. Knockout searches run Nov 7, 2026 (App Store search API, whois/HTTP, web search). These are knockouts, not legal clearance.

Rhyme

WINNER

Two lines that end alike but aren't identical. Fit without sameness; 'rhyme and reason' is the idiom for exactly our promise (a fit, with a reason).

Knockout: Apple App Store US: no dating app named Rhyme in top results (itinerary, rap, dictionary, game apps only). rhyme.com is held by a topic-based social platform (since 1997). rhymeapp.com, tryrhyme.com, joinrhyme.com are registered. meetrhyme.com showed no registration. A near-sound 'Ryme' (activity-based matching app) appeared in web results.

Risk: Medium. Class 9/45 clearance search + attorney review needed, especially vs. 'Ryme'. Mitigate: always use full word, rhyme.dating style domain, distinct mark.

Couplet

Runner-up

Two lines that rhyme as a pair. Warm, bookish, 'couple' built in.

Knockout: Apple App Store US: 'Couplet: Spice Up Relationship' and 'Couplet Confessions' (Lifestyle, same developer), plus couplet.love. couplet.com is held by MarkMonitor (302 redirect). couplet.dating returns no website.

Risk: High. Direct relationship-app overlap in adjacent class; confusion likely in App Store search.

Tessera

Alternate

A single mosaic tile: it only means something beside the one that fits. Quiet, premium.

Knockout: Apple App Store US: only games, a personal assistant app and a music tool; no dating app. tessera.app is live (other owner). Weak link to 'reason'; harder to spell and say.

Risk: Medium-low in dating; weaker brand story and hard to say out loud. Fallback if Rhyme clears badly.

(Eliminated) Dovetail

Out

Joinery where each side is cut to fit the other.

Knockout: A live dating app already exists (dovetail.dating) with a similar slow, one-match-a-day idea. Dovetail.com is a large research software company.

Risk: Blocked. Do not use.

(Eliminated) Twofold / Ditto / Latch

Out

Considered for 'both ways'.

Knockout: Twofold: 6+ unrelated App Store apps incl. a couples app (Lifestyle) and active trytwofold.com. Ditto: many social apps incl. 'Ditto: Fun Social Media'. Latch: smart-lock company (Latchable).

Risk: Crowded. Do not use.

Pick: Rhyme. A rhyme is two lines that are not the same but belong together, and 'rhyme and reason' is the idiom for the promise. It is a short, soft, speakable word. Suggested handles/domains to check or buy: rhyme.dating, meetrhyme.com (no registration seen), getrhyme.com, @rhymeapp. Do not rely on rhyme.com / rhymeapp.com.

02 / LOGO

Two lines. Same ending.

The mark is a couplet: a long bar and a short bar that stop on the same right edge. Different lengths, same landing. It reads as two sides arriving together, as two chat bubbles, as two transit lines. The wordmark is lowercase Fraunces (SemiBold, optical size 144) drawn to outlines, tightened 14 units per glyph.

Rules

  • Clear space: one bar-height (28 units of the mark) on all sides.
  • Minimum size: mark 16 px / 6 mm; lockup 72 px wide / 20 mm.
  • Light: Plum bars with a Tangerine short bar on Paper. Dark: Paper long bar, Lime short bar on Plum Ink.
  • 1-color: both bars same color. For stamps, emboss, one-ink print.
  • Never: tilt, outline, add a gradient, re-space the bars, align the bars to the left, or swap the long and short bars.

06 / MOTION

Land together.

The motion principle: two things move differently and arrive at the same place. One element is long and slow, one short and quick (or the reverse), both ease-out and stop on a shared edge. Use it for the logo animation, screen transitions between 'you' and 'them', loading, and the reveal of a reason.

  • Duration 450-900 ms, ease-out (cubic-bezier .2,.8,.2,1). No bounce, no overshoot.
  • Offset start by 120-250 ms; they never finish apart.
  • On a successful mutual yes: both bars settle, one soft haptic, nothing else. No confetti.
  • Reduce Motion: replace with a 150 ms fade.
07 / IMAGE STYLE

Pairs, not portraits.

Photography

  • Diptychs: two frames, two different people or places, one shared horizon line or one shared object (the 'rhyme').
  • Real New York, daylight, flat light, a little grain; stoops, train windows, bookstores, bodegas, rooftops.
  • People caught doing, not posing: reading, cooking, waiting. No staged laughing at salads, no couples kissing.
  • Faces optional. Hands, shoes and shared details often tell the match better.
  • Casting: real New Yorkers 21-39, range of body, age, race, orientation and gender. Model releases required.

Illustration

  • Built from the pill: rounded bars with a long and a short version always ending on the same edge.
  • Flat color from the palette, 2 colors max per piece, no outlines or textures.
  • Used for UI states, empty screens, event graphics and ads when photos can't carry it.

08 / VOICE AND TONE

The friend who tells you the truth, with a rhyme budget.

Keeps the platform voice from the existing BRAND_AND_COPY: plain, warm, dry, specific, honest about being new. Rhyme adds one rule: one rhyme-pun per surface, never in a headline and the CTA at the same time.

Say
  • "We only introduce you when it fits you both."
  • "Each introduction comes with a reason."
  • "We're just starting in New York."
  • "Nobody pays to be seen."
Don't say
  • "Find your perfect match" / "soulmate" / "the one".
  • "Thousands of singles", "proven", "verified" (until true).
  • Rhyme and chime puns stacked: "Time to find your rhyme!"
  • Exclamation marks. Emoji strings. Competitor names.
